French press coffee: ratio, grind and brew time

Coffee from a French press is rich and heavy because nothing but a metal mesh stands between the grounds and your cup. Getting it right comes down to three things: the ratio of coffee to water, the grind size and the brew time.

The ratio

For a standard 1-litre French press, use 60 grams of coffee and 900 grams of water. If the coffee tastes weak, add more coffee rather than brewing for longer.

The grind

Use a coarse, even grind. Blade grinders produce a lot of fine dust that slips through the filter, so a burr grinder is worth it if you brew this way every day.

The brew time

Four minutes is the classic French press steep. Some baristas prefer a longer steep followed by skimming the crust and waiting before pressing, which gives a cleaner cup. Try both and see which you like.

Cleaning

Rinse the French press after every use and wash the mesh filter with warm, soapy water each week.
