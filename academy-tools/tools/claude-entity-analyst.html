What this can and cannot tell you

  • It is an analyst, not a measurement. Google's NLP API gives salience scores you can re-run; Claude gives judgement — implied entities, relationships, missing attributes. Use both: the Entity Extractor for the numbers, this for what extraction cannot see.
  • Answers vary between runs and models. The fixed output shape makes them comparable, not identical. Record the model and date with any finding.
  • Missing attributes are suggestions. They come from the model's general knowledge of what readers expect — check them against your gap matrix and real queries before you brief anyone.
  • Long pages are trimmed to the first 30,000 characters. Feed it the main content, not the whole HTML.
  • Your content goes to Anthropic. Check your client agreement before you paste client pages. Nothing is sent to MLforSEO.
